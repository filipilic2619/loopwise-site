Forums for Confluence

A discussion forum in every Confluence space, built on real pages and comments.

Coming soon to the Atlassian MarketplaceGet early accessQuestions? Contact support
  • Topics in categories: with the latest activity first, topics without replies and your own topics.
  • Replies and likes: replies are Confluence comments, so notifications and search just work.
  • Search: finds words in titles, topics and replies.
  • Moderation: pin, lock and move topics; space admins choose categories and moderators.
  • Forum macro: shows the latest topics on any page.

Getting started

  1. Install the app from the Atlassian Marketplace (coming soon).
  2. Open a space and choose Forum under Space apps.
  3. In Space settings → Integrations → Forum, add categories and moderators.
  4. Click New topic.

FAQ

Who can see topics? Everyone who can see the space; topics follow the space's permissions.

Where is content stored? In Confluence: topics are pages and replies are comments.
