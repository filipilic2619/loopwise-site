Confluence Export (PDF & Word)

Export a Confluence page, or a whole page tree, to PDF or Word with images.

Coming soon to the Atlassian MarketplaceGet early accessQuestions? Contact support
  • Page or page tree: the current page, or the page with all its child pages (up to 300).
  • Proper documents: cover page, table of contents, numbered titles and page breaks.
  • Images included: attached images, tables, code blocks, panels and lists.
  • Download or attach: keep the file on the page as a record.
  • Stores nothing: documents are made in your browser with your permissions.

Getting started

  1. Install the app from the Atlassian Marketplace (coming soon).
  2. On a page, open ••• → Apps → Export to PDF / Word.
  3. Choose this page or the page tree, the format and the options.
  4. Download, or attach the file to the page.

FAQ

How many pages? Up to 300 pages in one export.

What is stored? Nothing. The app has no storage.
