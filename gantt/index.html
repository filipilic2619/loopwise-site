Gantt Chart for Jira

See every project, or several at once, on one timeline. Drag to reschedule. Nothing you didn't touch ever changes.

Find it on the Atlassian MarketplaceQuestions? Contact support
  • Hierarchy on the left: epics, stories and sub-tasks with expand and collapse, assignee, start and due.
  • Drag to move or resize: the new dates are saved to Jira at once, as you, with your permissions. Ctrl+Z undoes it. No save button to forget.
  • Dependencies: Blocks links drawn as arrows; a successor that starts before its predecessor ends turns red.
  • Milestones: release dates of your versions, a today line and weekend shading. Zoom by day, week, month or quarter.
  • Plans across projects: combine projects or any JQL into a saved plan, private or shared.
  • Safe by design: the app only ever changes the start and due date of an issue you drag. It never deletes issues, links or anything else, and stores no issue data.

Getting started

  1. Install the app from the Atlassian Marketplace.
  2. Open a project and choose Gantt in the project navigation, or open Apps → Gantt plans to combine several projects.
  3. Drag bars to reschedule. Issues without dates are listed too: click on their row in the chart to schedule them.

FAQ

Which dates are used? Jira's Start date and Due date by default. You can pick any date fields in Settings. Issues without dates can use their sprint's dates.

Why can't I move a bar? You need permission to edit the issue, and the date fields must be on the issue type's edit screen.

How many issues? Up to 5,000 per chart; they load page by page, so the first rows appear right away.
