Cost & Budget Tracker for Jira

Turn the time your team logs in Jira into money: hourly rates, project budgets and what's left.

Coming soon to the Atlassian MarketplaceGet early accessQuestions? Contact support
  • Budgets: for a project, several projects or any JQL, with an amount and an optional period: spent, forecast and remaining, plus On track or Over budget.
  • Rates per person: a default rate and a rate per person, each with the date it starts, so past work keeps its price.
  • Costs page in every project: cost per person, per month and per issue, with sub-tasks rolled up.
  • Private by default: only Jira admins and the people they list can see rates and amounts.
  • Read-only: costs come from Jira worklogs; the app never changes your issues and stores no issue data.

Getting started

  1. Install the app from the Atlassian Marketplace (coming soon).
  2. Open Apps → Cost & budgets and set your currency and rates in Rates & settings.
  3. Click Create budget, pick projects or a JQL, and enter the amount.
  4. Open any project's Costs page for the details.

FAQ

Where does the cost come from? Time logged in Jira (worklogs) times the person's rate on that day. The forecast adds remaining estimates.

Who can see money? Jira admins, plus the people they add in settings. Everyone else sees no amounts.
