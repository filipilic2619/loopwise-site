Planning Poker for Jira

Estimate together in Jira, live in a meeting or at your own pace, and save the result to story points.

Coming soon to the Atlassian MarketplaceGet early accessQuestions? Contact support
  • Sessions from JQL: or just the issues without story points; Fibonacci, T-shirt or your own cards.
  • Secret votes: hidden until the facilitator reveals them, enforced by the app.
  • Reveal: average, lowest, highest and the closest card.
  • Saved to Jira: the agreed estimate goes straight into story points.
  • Async voting: from the Planning poker panel on each issue in a session.

Getting started

  1. Install the app from the Atlassian Marketplace (coming soon).
  2. Open a project and choose Planning poker.
  3. Click New session, pick the issues and start.
  4. Vote, reveal, and accept to save story points.

FAQ

Can people vote without a meeting? Yes, from the Planning poker panel on each issue in an open session.

What is stored? Sessions, voters' account IDs and their votes. Closed accounts are removed automatically every week.
