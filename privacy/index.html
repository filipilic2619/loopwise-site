Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

This policy explains how Filip Ilić PR Agencija za računarsko programiranje Filip Code Kaona, registered in the Republic of Serbia, company ID (MB) 65422174, tax ID (PIB) 111454976, seat: Kaona, Serbia, operating under the brand Loopwise Labs ("Loopwise Labs", "we"), handles data in the Atlassian Marketplace apps Recurring Issues for Jira, Q&A for Confluence, Page Approval for Confluence, Epic Progress for Jira, Issue Templates for Jira, Sprint Capacity for Jira and Sprint Burndown & Burnup for Jira (each an "App").

1. Where the App runs

The App is built on Atlassian Forge and runs entirely on Atlassian's infrastructure. It does not send data to servers operated by us or by any third party.

2. What the Apps store

Recurring Issues for Jira stores in Atlassian Forge storage, for each recurring rule your users create:

  • summary and description templates;
  • project ID, issue type ID, labels and schedule (time, time zone, dates);
  • the Atlassian account ID of the user who created the rule and, if set, of the assignee;
  • run status: the last created issue key, timestamps and the last error message.

Q&A for Confluence creates ordinary Confluence content on behalf of your users (questions as pages, answers as comments, labels for tags and status). In addition it stores:

  • in page properties: the account IDs of users who voted and of the user who accepted an answer;
  • in Atlassian Forge storage: per space, the account IDs of moderators and the FAQ order.

Page Approval for Confluence stores in page properties the approval status and history of each page: the account IDs of the requester and approvers, their decisions and comments, page versions, dates and an optional note. In Atlassian Forge storage it keeps per space the account IDs of default approvers and approval managers. It also adds status labels and posts comments that @mention approvers and requesters.

Epic Progress for Jira stores no data. It reads issues as the viewing user to show progress and keeps nothing afterwards.

Issue Templates for Jira stores in Atlassian Forge storage the templates your users write (names, texts, issue type, priority and label names, sub-task texts) and one setting. It stores no account IDs.

Sprint Capacity for Jira stores in Atlassian Forge storage, per board and sprint, daily rates and days off per person together with their Atlassian account ID. It reads sprint issues to calculate totals and does not store issue content.

Sprint Burndown & Burnup for Jira stores no data apart from the gadget settings Jira keeps for the dashboard (board, sprint, measure).

The Apps do not store names or email addresses. Apart from Atlassian account IDs, the only personal data is free text your users choose to enter (such as approval notes and comments).

3. What we can access

We do not have access to your Jira or Confluence content or the Apps' stored data. The exception is function logs that Atlassian provides to app developers. These contain technical information (IDs, issue keys, error messages) and are used only to diagnose problems.

4. Purpose and legal basis

The data is processed only to provide the Apps' functionality, such as creating issues on the schedule you configure running questions and answers or approvals in your spaces (performance of a contract).

5. Retention and deletion

App data stays until it is deleted by your users or the App is uninstalled. After uninstall, Atlassian deletes the App's Forge storage under its own retention rules. Questions, answers and approval comments created by the Apps are your Confluence content and remain in your spaces until you delete them. Once a week each App asks Atlassian which stored accounts have been closed and removes those account IDs from its data.

6. Sub-processors

Atlassian (hosting via Forge). See the Atlassian Privacy Policy.

7. Your rights

Depending on your location (for example under the GDPR), you may have rights of access, correction, deletion and objection. Because the Apps store data only inside your Atlassian site, most requests can be fulfilled by deleting the relevant rules or content. Otherwise, contact us.

8. Data Processing Agreement

For customers subject to the GDPR, our Data Processing Agreement applies automatically to the processing described above.
