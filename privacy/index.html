Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

This policy explains how Filip Ilić PR Agencija za računarsko programiranje Filip Code Kaona, registered in the Republic of Serbia, company ID (MB) 65422174, tax ID (PIB) 111454976, operating under the brand Loopwise Labs ("Loopwise Labs", "we"), handles data in the Atlassian Marketplace apps Recurring Issues for Jira, Q&A for Confluence, Page Approval for Confluence, Epic Progress for Jira, Issue Templates for Jira, Sprint Capacity for Jira, Sprint Burndown & Burnup for Jira, Cascading Select for Jira, Gantt Chart for Jira, Capacity Planner for Jira, Cost & Budget Tracker for Jira, Markdown for Confluence, Roadmaps for Jira, Issue Export for Jira (PDF & Word), Grid Field for Jira, Read Confirmations for Confluence, Forums for Confluence, Planning Poker for Jira, CRM for Jira and Page Export for Confluence (PDF & Word) (each an "App").

1. Where the App runs

The App is built on Atlassian Forge and runs entirely on Atlassian's infrastructure. It does not send data to servers operated by us or by any third party.

2. What the Apps store

Recurring Issues for Jira stores in Atlassian Forge storage, for each recurring rule your users create:

  • summary and description templates;
  • project ID, issue type ID, labels and schedule (time, time zone, dates);
  • the Atlassian account ID of the user who created the rule and, if set, of the assignee;
  • run status: the last created issue key, timestamps and the last error message.

Q&A for Confluence creates ordinary Confluence content on behalf of your users (questions as pages, answers as comments, labels for tags and status). In addition it stores:

  • in page properties: the account IDs of users who voted and of the user who accepted an answer;
  • in Atlassian Forge storage: per space, the account IDs of moderators and the FAQ order.

Page Approval for Confluence stores in page properties the approval status and history of each page: the account IDs of the requester and approvers, their decisions and comments, page versions, dates and an optional note. In Atlassian Forge storage it keeps per space the account IDs of default approvers and approval managers. It also adds status labels and posts comments that @mention approvers and requesters.

Epic Progress for Jira stores no data. It reads issues as the viewing user to show progress and keeps nothing afterwards.

Issue Templates for Jira stores in Atlassian Forge storage the templates your users write (names, texts, issue type, priority and label names, sub-task texts) and one setting. It stores no account IDs.

Sprint Capacity for Jira stores in Atlassian Forge storage, per board and sprint, daily rates and days off per person together with their Atlassian account ID. It reads sprint issues to calculate totals and does not store issue content.

Cascading Select for Jira stores the option tree an admin enters (in the field configuration and in Forge storage) and the chosen values as ordinary Jira field values. It stores no personal data.

Sprint Burndown & Burnup for Jira stores no data apart from the gadget settings Jira keeps for the dashboard (board, sprint, measure).

Gantt Chart for Jira stores in Atlassian Forge storage the plans users save (name, chosen projects or JQL, display settings, and the Atlassian account ID of the plan's creator) and per-user view settings (zoom and collapsed rows) keyed by Atlassian account ID. Issue data is read from Jira in the user's browser when the chart is shown and is never stored. The only change the App makes in Jira is to the start and due dates of issues the user drags.

Capacity Planner for Jira stores in Atlassian Forge storage the teams users create (name, the Atlassian account IDs of the creator and members, chosen projects or JQL, unit and public holidays), each person's capacity settings keyed by Atlassian account ID (hours or story points per day, working weekdays and time-off dates with an optional note), and planned allocations (person, label, dates and percentage). Issue data is read from Jira in the user's browser when the grid is shown and is never stored. The App does not change anything in Jira.

Cost & Budget Tracker for Jira stores in Atlassian Forge storage its settings (currency, default rate and the Atlassian account IDs of people allowed to see costs), hourly rates per person keyed by Atlassian account ID with the date each rate starts, and budgets (name, owner account ID, projects or JQL, amount and period). Worklogs and issues are read from Jira when costs are shown and are never stored. The App does not change anything in Jira.

Markdown for Confluence stores no data. It reads content as the current user, in the browser, and keeps nothing afterwards.

Roadmaps for Jira stores in Atlassian Forge storage saved roadmaps (name, owner Atlassian account ID, projects or JQL and display settings) and each user's view settings (zoom and collapsed rows) keyed by Atlassian account ID. Issue data is read from Jira when the roadmap is shown and is never stored. The App only changes the start and due date of an epic that a user drags.

Issue Export for Jira (PDF & Word) stores no data. It reads content as the current user, in the browser, and keeps nothing afterwards.

Grid Field for Jira stores no data. It reads content as the current user, in the browser, and keeps nothing afterwards.

Read Confirmations for Confluence stores, in a content property on each page or blog post with a request, the Atlassian account IDs of the requester and of the readers, which page version each reader confirmed and when, and the request's due date and note; and in Atlassian Forge storage per-space settings with the account IDs of default readers and managers. Closed accounts are removed automatically every week.

Forums for Confluence stores in Atlassian Forge storage each space's forum categories and the Atlassian account IDs of its moderators, and in a content property on each topic page the account IDs of people who liked the topic or a reply, plus its pinned and locked state. Topics and replies are regular Confluence pages and comments. Closed accounts are removed automatically every week.

Planning Poker for Jira stores in Atlassian Forge storage estimation sessions (name, issue keys, cards, the Atlassian account ID of the facilitator and the agreed estimates) and each vote (issue key, voter account ID and the card). Issue data is read from Jira when a session is shown and is never stored. When a facilitator accepts an estimate, it is saved to the issue's story points as that user. Closed accounts are removed automatically every week.

CRM for Jira stores in Atlassian Forge storage the companies, contacts and deals that users enter, including contact details of customers' people (name, email, phone, job title and notes), the Atlassian account IDs of record owners and note authors, and settings with the account IDs of people allowed to use the CRM. Links between issues and customers are stored as an issue property in Jira. Closed Atlassian accounts are removed automatically every week. The Customer is responsible for having a lawful basis for the contact details it enters.

Page Export for Confluence (PDF & Word) stores no data. It reads content as the current user, in the browser, and keeps nothing afterwards.

Apart from CRM for Jira, the Apps do not store names or email addresses. Apart from Atlassian account IDs, the only personal data is free text your users choose to enter (such as approval notes, comments and notes). CRM for Jira stores the contact details of the Customer's own customers that users enter (name, email, phone, job title and notes); the Customer decides what to enter and is responsible for having a lawful basis for it.

3. What we can access

We do not have access to your Jira or Confluence content or the Apps' stored data. The exception is function logs that Atlassian provides to app developers. These contain technical information (IDs, issue keys, error messages) and are used only to diagnose problems.

4. Purpose and legal basis

The data is processed only to provide the Apps' functionality, such as creating issues on the schedule you configure running questions and answers or approvals in your spaces (performance of a contract).

5. Retention and deletion

App data stays until it is deleted by your users or the App is uninstalled. After uninstall, Atlassian deletes the App's Forge storage under its own retention rules. Questions, answers and approval comments created by the Apps are your Confluence content and remain in your spaces until you delete them. Once a week each App asks Atlassian which stored accounts have been closed and removes those account IDs from its data.

6. Sub-processors

Atlassian (hosting via Forge). See the Atlassian Privacy Policy.

7. Your rights

Depending on your location (for example under the GDPR), you may have rights of access, correction, deletion and objection. Because the Apps store data only inside your Atlassian site, most requests can be fulfilled by deleting the relevant rules or content. Otherwise, contact us.

8. Data Processing Agreement

For customers subject to the GDPR, our Data Processing Agreement applies automatically to the processing described above.

9. This website

loopwiselabs.app uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to count page views. It sets no cookies, does not track you across sites and does not collect personal data; it records only aggregate data such as the page, referring site, country and browser type. Purchases of the Forge Starter Kit are handled by Gumroad under its own privacy policy.
