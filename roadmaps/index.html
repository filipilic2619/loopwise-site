Roadmaps for Jira

All your epics on one clean timeline, across projects, with progress and release milestones. No Jira Premium needed.

Coming soon to the Atlassian MarketplaceGet early accessQuestions? Contact support
  • Across projects: a Roadmap page in every project, plus saved roadmaps that combine projects or any JQL, private or shared.
  • Lanes: by project, label or component, so each team sees its own row of epics.
  • Real progress: from the epic's child issues: done, in progress and to do.
  • Dates from children: epics without dates use the dates of their child issues.
  • Milestones: release dates and a today line; zoom by month or quarter.
  • Drag to reschedule: new dates are saved to Jira as you, with your permissions.

Getting started

  1. Install the app from the Atlassian Marketplace (coming soon).
  2. Open a project and choose Roadmap, or open Apps → Roadmaps for a roadmap across projects.
  3. Pick how to group epics into lanes.
  4. Drag an epic to change its dates.

FAQ

Do I need Jira Premium? No. It works on every Jira Cloud plan.

What does the app change? Only the start and due date of an epic you drag. It never deletes anything.
