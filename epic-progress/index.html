Epic Progress for Jira

How far along is this epic, really? Issues, story points and time in one place, without building a dashboard.

Find it on the Atlassian MarketplaceQuestions? Contact support
  • On every epic: a Progress panel with done, in progress and to do, story points done, and time logged, remaining and estimated.
  • Sub-tasks included: time tracking uses Jira's aggregate fields, so work logged on sub-tasks counts.
  • What needs attention: overdue, unassigned and unestimated issues are flagged.
  • All epics of a project on one page: progress bars, points, time left and warnings, sorted by least progress or most work left.
  • Works for stories too: any issue with sub-tasks gets the same roll-up.
  • Stores nothing: the app only reads Jira, as the person viewing it, so everyone sees only what they already may see.

Getting started

  1. Install the app from the Atlassian Marketplace.
  2. Open an epic and expand Progress on the right.
  3. For a project overview, open the Epic progress tab in the project.

FAQ

Which story points field is used? The app finds "Story Points" and "Story point estimate" automatically, in company-managed and team-managed projects.

How is time shown? In Jira's format with 8-hour days and 5-day weeks.

What happens if my subscription ends? The panel and page show a notice until the license is active again. Nothing is stored, so there is nothing to clean up.
