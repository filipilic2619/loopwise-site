CRM for Jira

A simple CRM inside Jira: companies, contacts and deals, linked to the issues you work on.

Coming soon to the MarketplaceGet early access
  • Companies, contacts and deals With owners, values, stages, tags and notes.
  • Pipeline Deals by stage with totals; move a deal to the next stage in one click.
  • Linked to Jira A CRM panel on every issue, and each customer lists its issues; search with crmCompany = "id".
  • CSV import and export Bring contacts and companies from a spreadsheet or another CRM.
  • Your setup Currency, stage names and who can use the CRM.

Getting started

  1. Install the app from the Atlassian Marketplace (coming soon).
  2. Open Apps → CRM and add a company, or import a CSV.
  3. Create deals and move them through the pipeline.
  4. On an issue, use the CRM panel to link a customer.

FAQ

Where is the data stored? Inside Atlassian, in the app's Forge storage. Nothing leaves Atlassian.

Who can use it? Everyone in Jira by default; admins can limit it to listed people.

Questions, or want early access? Contact support.
