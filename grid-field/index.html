Grid Field for Jira

A table on every Jira issue: your columns, rows, totals, and paste straight from Excel.

Coming soon to the MarketplaceGet early access
  • Your columns Up to 15, of type text, number, select, date or checkbox, with required columns and a row limit.
  • Paste from Excel Copy a block of cells from Excel or Google Sheets into the grid; 1.234,5 and 3.4.2026 are understood.
  • Totals For number columns, shown on the issue.
  • JQL search By number of rows and total, for example "Order items.Total" > 1000.
  • Native field On create, edit and transition screens in company-managed projects.

Getting started

  1. Install the app from the Atlassian Marketplace (coming soon).
  2. In Settings → Work items → Fields, create a field of type Grid / table (Loopwise) and add it to your screens.
  3. Open the field's context and Edit custom field config to choose the columns.
  4. On an issue, click the field to add rows.

FAQ

Where are the rows stored? In Jira itself, as the field's value on the issue. The app only keeps the column setup.

Team-managed projects? Jira supports app fields in company-managed projects.

Questions, or want early access? Contact support.
