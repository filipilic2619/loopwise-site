Gantt chart for multiple Jira projects: your options in Jira Cloud

One project on a timeline is easy in Jira. Several projects, with dependencies between them and dates you can drag, is where teams start looking for a Gantt chart. Here is what Jira Cloud offers and when an app makes sense.

Option 1: The project Timeline

Every software project has a Timeline view with epics and child issues as bars.

Good for: one team, one project, epic-level planning.

Watch out for: it shows one project at a time, and the detail below epics is limited.

Option 2: Plans (Jira Premium)

Plans (formerly Advanced Roadmaps) combines several projects and boards, with dependencies and capacity.

Good for: organisations already on Premium that need portfolio planning.

Watch out for: it requires the Premium plan for everyone on the site, and it takes setup and training.

Option 3: A Gantt app

A Gantt app gives you a classic chart across projects on any Jira plan. Gantt Chart for Jira is ours:

  • Several projects or any JQL in one saved plan, private or shared.
  • Hierarchy: epics, stories and sub-tasks with expand and collapse.
  • Drag to reschedule: new dates are saved to Jira at once, with your permissions, and Ctrl+Z undoes it.
  • Dependencies: "blocks" links drawn as arrows; a successor that starts too early turns red.
  • Milestones: release dates, a today line, and zoom by day, week, month or quarter.
  • Safe by design: it only changes the start and due dates of issues you drag, and never deletes anything.

Which one should you use?

  • One project, epics only: the built-in Timeline.
  • Portfolio planning on Premium: Plans.
  • Several projects on Standard, or issue-level Gantt with dependencies: a Gantt app.
