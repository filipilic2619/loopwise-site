Q&A for Confluence

Your team's questions and answers, inside Confluence, where the knowledge already lives.

Find it on the Atlassian MarketplaceQuestions? Contact support
  • Search that finds answers: questions are real Confluence pages and answers are comments, so Confluence search covers both, not just titles.
  • Permissions you already have: each space's permissions decide who can see, ask and answer.
  • Notifications for free: watch a question and Confluence tells you about new answers.
  • Accepted answers and voting: the asker, or a space moderator, can accept the best answer.
  • FAQ view: solved questions in an order your moderators choose. No points or leaderboards.
  • Tags and an Unanswered queue so nothing gets lost.
  • Embed anywhere: add the Questions macro to any page, with an Ask button.
  • Your content stays yours: everything is ordinary Confluence content, so it stays if you uninstall.

Getting started

  1. Install the app from the Atlassian Marketplace.
  2. In a space, open Space apps → Questions in the sidebar and click Ask a question.
  3. Optional: space admins can pick moderators under Space settings → Q&A.
  4. Optional: add the Questions (Q&A) macro to any page.

FAQ

Where are questions stored? As pages under a "Questions" page in the space, with answers as comments. Votes and the accepted answer are stored as page properties.

A new question doesn't show in the FAQ yet. Confluence search can take a few seconds to index changes.

What happens if my subscription ends? Everything stays readable in Confluence. Asking, answering and voting through the app pause until the license is active again.
