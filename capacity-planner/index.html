Capacity Planner for Jira

See who has room and who is overloaded in the coming weeks, straight from your Jira estimates. No timesheets, no other app needed.

Coming soon to the Atlassian MarketplaceGet early accessQuestions? Contact support
  • Week-by-week heat map: one row per person, one column per week, load against capacity, colored from "has room" to "over capacity". Team totals and unassigned work underneath.
  • The issues behind every number: click a cell to see which issues and planned time make up that week.
  • Real capacity: focus hours or story points per day, working weekdays, time off and public holidays.
  • Plan time: reserve part of someone's week for work that is not in Jira yet, such as "50% on discovery in March".
  • Teams across projects: all of a team's work, only some projects, or any JQL. Every project also gets a Capacity page that needs no setup.
  • Read-only: the app never changes your issues. It stores no issue data.

Getting started

  1. Install the app from the Atlassian Marketplace.
  2. Open a project and choose Capacity in the project navigation, or open Apps → Capacity planner and create a team.
  3. Click a person to set their hours per day, working days and time off.

How load is calculated

Each open issue's remaining estimate (or story points) is spread evenly over the person's working days between its start date and due date. When those are missing, the sprint's start and end are used. Only work from today on counts, so overdue work shows up as load this week. Issues with an estimate but no dates or sprint are listed under No dates.

FAQ

Do I need Tempo or another time tracking app? No. Everything comes from Jira fields: remaining estimate, story points, start date, due date and sprints.

Who can change capacity? Each person can change their own. The person who created a team can change it for its members.

How many issues? Up to 5,000 per team view.
