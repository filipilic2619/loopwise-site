Sprint Burndown & Burnup for Jira

The sprint chart your team actually looks at, right on the Jira dashboard.

  • Burndown or burnup for the active sprint (follows the board) or any chosen sprint.
  • Your measure: story points, issue count or original estimate in hours.
  • On track or behind: compares completed work with the guideline and shows days left.
  • Weekends skipped by default, so the guideline is realistic.
  • Stores nothing: the gadget reads Jira and shows totals only.

Getting started

  1. Install the app from the Atlassian Marketplace.
  2. On a Jira dashboard click Edit → Add gadget → Sprint Burndown & Burnup.
  3. Choose the board, measure and chart type, and save.

FAQ

Issues added mid-sprint? The chart uses the sprint's current issues for the whole sprint.
