Read Confirmations for Confluence

Ask people to confirm they've read a page, and ask again when it changes.

Coming soon to the MarketplaceGet early access
  • Requests on any page Or blog post, with readers, a due date and a note.
  • One-click confirm Right on the page.
  • Re-confirmation When the page changes, everyone confirms the new version again.
  • Full record Who confirmed which version and when, exportable to CSV.
  • Space overview Every open and completed request in one place, with default readers set by space managers.

Getting started

  1. Install the app from the Atlassian Marketplace (coming soon).
  2. On a page, click Read confirmations in the byline and request confirmations.
  3. Readers confirm with one click.
  4. Follow progress in the space's Read confirmations page.

FAQ

Where is the record kept? In a content property on the page itself, so it moves with the page.

Blog posts? Yes, pages and blog posts both work.
